Google Research · AI & TPU Silicon

Hodjat Asghari Esfeden, Ph.D.

Senior Silicon & AI Engineer — Agentic Chip Design, TPU Silicon & EDA Benchmarks

I work at Google Research (in partnership with AI & TPU Silicon) building autonomous spec-to-silicon multi-agent flows, simulation-in-the-loop debugging agents, and execution-grounded evaluation benchmarks over commercial EDA toolchains. Over 6+ years at Google, I have contributed across multiple Google TPU generations spanning block-level verification, SoC execution, cloud-scale EDA simulation infrastructure, and AI-native hardware workflows.

I received my Ph.D. and M.Sc. in Computer Science from UC Riverside under Prof. Nael Abu-Ghazaleh, and my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.

Focus

Agentic Chip Design

Spec-to-silicon multi-agent flows, autonomous RTL/UVM code review, waveform debugging agents, and execution-grounded EDA benchmarks.

GPU & TPU Architecture

TPU interconnect verification, cloud-scale EDA infrastructure, and GPU register data-flow & task-based execution.

Hardware Security

Microarchitectural defenses against transient execution attacks (SpecASan) and last-level cache side channels (RIC).
